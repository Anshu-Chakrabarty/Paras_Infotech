Who can Search, Submit and Download

These are the people you kept on the KEKA map. We have filled Search, Submit, Download and Approve from the portal role you already picked. Tick or untick if that person should get something different.

How to read the four ticks

Search — open the list, filter plant and date, look at a row. Every login usually has this.

Submit — type / edit. What they type depends on the role (shown on each group).

Download — Excel and PDF of the open list or row.

Approve — lock a submitted Daily or Monthly, or send it back with a reason.
